## 3. Reddit (r/selfhosted, r/homelab, r/devops)

```markdown
**Title:** I built Fleet OS — a lightweight, self-healing container cluster for the spare hardware sitting in your closet

Hey r/selfhosted!

Like many of you, I had retired machines (an old ThinkPad, a low-power mini PC, and a small Frankfurt VPS) sitting around while I kept paying cloud providers monthly bills for small personal services. Kubernetes at home felt like overkill (40+ YAML files and broken etcd clusters at 2 AM).

So I built **Fleet OS**:
- **One curl pair command:** Generates a secure WireGuard mesh overlay between all your nodes.
- **Dynamic Scheduler:** Distributes containers based on RAM, CPU architecture, and machine availability.
- **Fast Failover:** If an intermittent machine drops offline (e.g. laptop lid closes or power disconnects), services automatically reschedule in ~4.1 seconds.
- **Multi-Architecture:** Native multi-arch builds (`arm64`, `amd64`, `armv7`) out of the box.

It's completely open source and free to self-host:
- GitHub: https://github.com/YadurajManu/fleet-os
- Live Docs & Demo: https://fleet-os.dev

Would love your feedback and hardware pairing stories!
```
