POV: You have a drawer full of retired hardware doing nothing, while you pay AWS $68/mo for a hobby app with 4 users.

Fleet OS turns spare machines into an automated, self-healing cloud. One curl command, WireGuard encrypted mesh, 4s zero-downtime failover when someone unplugs the laptop.
